English

Cabinette does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, no servers. The app works entirely on your device. Your collections, photos and settings are stored only on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them).
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. Cabinette contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to add a photo; images stay on your device.
  • Face ID / passcode is handled by iOS; Cabinette never sees your biometric data.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We receive no payment or personal information.
  • Sharing happens only when you choose to export or share a file, and only with the recipients you pick.

Contact: cabinette.support@icloud.com

Deutsch

Cabinette erhebt, speichert und teilt keine personenbezogenen Daten.

  • Kein Konto, keine Server. Die App läuft vollständig auf deinem Gerät. Sammlungen, Fotos und Einstellungen werden nur auf deinem Gerät gespeichert (und in deinen Geräte-Backups, falls du sie nutzt).
